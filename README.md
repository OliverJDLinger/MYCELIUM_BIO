# MYCELIUM_BIO
Finding ways and modelling ways in which micelial networks can advance human function.
## Key goals
1. Build a model on how mycelium is similar to human neurological function.
2. Demonstrate hypothesis on how mycelial networks can be used to improve the human body.
3. visualise the benfits to the human system using said hypothesis.

The goal is to demostrate fields of study that can be used to advance human function.
Humans are forever looking at the benfits of robotics, the substitution of human function and rarely the safe accelerattion of human function.
The goal is not to replace humans, but to offer a structured area of study focused on advancing our human experience of the multi dimensional space we inhabit. 

We seem to have forgotten that human experience should be advanced and not replaced. The goal here is to push the bounds of what we can do so that it we can actively compete with the tools we create. 
If we advance alongside our tools, we hopefeully will have the avility to understand them better. A society that doesnt understand the tools it uses, loses the ability to correct them.
