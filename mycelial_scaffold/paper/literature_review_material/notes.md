## Paper with a simuating model
paper = https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9292515/#febs15832-sec-0005
this outlines a method for delineating stem cell rejuvination in other tissue types. 
It focuses on ways that paths acan be delineated for quiescent skeletal stem cell types to be rejuvinated in the aged. 
It helps identify niche signalling factors which can then be used to rejuvinate the quiescent stem cells.

We can use it as a tool to not just delineate paths but simulate stem cell growth in engineered tissue structures. Mycelial structures which are entirely biodegradable.
