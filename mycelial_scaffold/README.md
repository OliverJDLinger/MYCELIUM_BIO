# Mycelium growth model

## Introduction
A mycelium/hyphaie growth model, in python. 
The goal is to show the growth patterns of mycelium with different outcomes wanted.

## Key goals
Get a stable mycelium whose structure can be used in wound healing. 
It will mimic skin cell structures. 
With a focus on Pleurotus ostreatus and Ganoderma lucidum.
Reduce the amount of toxic products of these fungi. 

This will review prior research an build on it with a model.
